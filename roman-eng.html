Novel: One Last Cà phê

Berlin, fall 2025. Kim Collins, 23, a detective of Vietnamese descent, takes on two seemingly unrelated cases: an unfaithful wife and a missing journalist. What begins as routine work unravels into a dangerous web of blackmail, drugs and murder.

But while Kim sets his sights on the antagonist Bernhardt Bogardt, a ruthless pharmaceutical mogul, something unexpected happens: in Vietnam he meets Nong – and for the first time in his life the cool-headed detective loses his heart.

A story about justice and crime, about cultures that collide and connect, about Berlin and Saigon, London and New York. And about the universal power of love, which knows no boundaries – not even those between two men.

Between Love and Murder.
