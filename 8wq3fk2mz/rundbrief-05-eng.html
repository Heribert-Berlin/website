Waldemar the Owl: Volume 08 is here

And now we travel to Vietnam …

May I introduce Kim's Grand Prize:
Nong Phạm

A letter to my readers

Dear readers,

by now you know the agency from top to bottom. Today is about someone who works in no agency and is nevertheless one of the most important characters in the novel: Nong Phạm, 21 years old, from Mũi Né in Vietnam.

Nong is a tour guide. He shows holidaymakers his country, and he has a quality that is rare among tour guides: he remembers everything. When Kim tells him that he spent another week at his sister's in London, Nong asks: „Sunny, is that right?" He has remembered the name. Kim, the detective, has to admit that he himself remembered nothing at all: „What a shame on me."

The novel tells how the two come together in a way I particularly like. Kim is back from his holiday and sorts through his photos in Berlin. He discovers that a considerable share of them is devoted to Nong. He has had dinner with him only twice, never alone, always under the eyes of relatives and neighbours. The man who reads every signal as a detective discovers his own feelings in holiday photos.

Then he calculates: 2:22 p.m. in Berlin, 7:22 p.m. in Vietnam, just right. He has saved Nong's Zalo, the Vietnamese answer to WhatsApp. Still he hesitates. Without asking, it stays an unspoken no, he reasons, and without asking he has already lost. ABBA is playing in the background, „The Winner Takes It All", and with that the decision is made. He chooses a video call because he wants to see Nong's reaction at once.

The call is declined. Kim fights back tears. Then the phone vibrates, and Nong is on the line. He only had to dash out of the house into the shed, „you understand?" Kim understands everything. Nong knows about the two of them and keeps the necessary discretion between them and the rest of the world.

„I am waiting full of longing", he says. Then he makes a heart sign with his fingers, blows a kiss and hangs up before Kim can answer.

When Kim later puts his dead phone on the charger, a message from Nong is waiting: „Ngủ ngon nhé, em yêu." Sweet dreams, my darling. Plus 17 missed calls from Olga, but that is another story.

Later the two set their ringtones to the same song. Each is the grand prize for the other.

In the second volume Nong lives in Berlin and learns German, as you know from the last newsletter. How the story of Kim and Nong ends in the first book is in the book and in the e-book.

Yours, Heribert Lange
