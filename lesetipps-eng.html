Why this page?

Anyone who writes without a big publisher knows the problem: the stories are there, but hardly anyone gets to hear about them. So this is where I introduce fellow authors whose books, in my opinion, deserve more attention. Each author has a page of their own; one click on the introduction takes you there.

No paid advertising, nothing in return: just a personal recommendation from one reader to another.

Stefan Röser

Historical novels · German history

Novels set in the years after the First World War: no great battles, but the everyday lives of ordinary people on the farm, in the village and in the Berlin of the late Weimar Republic. His books are available in German.
